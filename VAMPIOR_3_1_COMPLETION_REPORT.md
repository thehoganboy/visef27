## Device calibration and evaluation

1. Install the IPA and select Stereo. Use the camera button to capture synchronized pairs of a rigid checkerboard or ChArUco board with measured square sizes. Take at least 15 pairs spanning the common field of view and several board poses. Share each JSON. Decode the two `pgmBase64` values as PGM images. Record the device and iOS version, pair IDs, lens positions, frame dimensions, factory extrinsics, and illumination. Discard pairs with different focus or frame configurations.
2. On a workstation, use OpenCV calibration with the pinhole radial/tangential model and the stream intrinsics. Fit `k1,k2,p1,p2,k3` for each camera at the capture focus. Use the factory rotation and millimeter translation in the exported column order to rectify held-out pairs. Measure reprojection and vertical epipolar RMS at the original capture scale and require at most 1 px for each. Independently measure preferably fronto-parallel planes at 50, 100, 200, and 300 cm. Rectify and measure held-out planes with the fitted distortion and known baseline. Use the measured camera-axis depth for each reference. The app rejects profiles with more than 5% baseline mismatch or held-out errors larger than 4 cm or 10% of the reference, whichever is larger. Calibration fitting and reference assessment are offline.
3. Save `StereoCalibration.json` under **Files → On My iPhone → VAMPIOR** and reselect Stereo. The profile keys are `pair` (ordered IDs), `left`, `right`, `baselineMM`, `reprojectionRMSPx`, `epipolarRMSPx`, and `references` (objects containing `measuredCM` and `stereoCM`). Each lens object contains `width`, `height`, `intrinsics` ([fx, fy, cx, cy]), `distortion` (five OpenCV coefficients), and `position` (exported focus). Capture settings that fail the live geometry and focus checks withhold metric depth.
4. Run repeated 10-second trials per mode at the four distances, in a clear corridor, with lateral and central obstacles, in low light, on low-texture surfaces, and with controlled motion. Use a sighted spotter. Extend trials to camera denial, backgrounding, pressure interruptions, and rapid mode changes. Refresh and share diagnostics CSV after each scene. Compare central target ROI against the independent distance. Report MAE, RMSE, valid coverage, confidence, FPS, P95 latency, label accuracy, correct corridor decisions, false AVAILABLE, unnecessary STOP, calibration residuals, and switch outcomes.

No physical benchmark, installation, or calibration result is claimed. Apple’s dual-camera depth API can provide relative disparity, which does not establish absolute scale for this experiment. The prototype uses physical camera streams and the checked calibration profile.
