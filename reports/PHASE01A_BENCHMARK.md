## 4. Hardware Limitations & Clear Boundary Statements

1. **CPU Execution Provider:** The desktop Python benchmark executes on an Intel x64 CPU. Real-world mobile deployments utilize Apple Neural Engine (ANE on iPhone 14 Pro) and Android NNAPI / Qualcomm NPU, which operate under different execution characteristics.
2. **True Lens-to-Ear Latency:** The measured 44.66 ms represents purely *algorithmic processing latency*. Full lens-to-ear latency includes:
   - Camera sensor exposure and ISP rolling shutter ($\approx 16-33$ ms)
   - Video capture buffer copy ($\approx 5-10$ ms)
   - Algorithmic processing ($\approx 45$ ms)
   - Audio OS buffer & Bluetooth audio A2DP latency ($\approx 40-150$ ms if wireless, $\approx 10-20$ ms if wired)
   - *Total lens-to-ear latency is estimated between $80-230$ ms depending on audio output modality.*
3. **Monocular Scale Ambiguity:** Neural depth from single monocular frames without LiDAR is inherently subject to scale ambiguity depending on camera tilt, mounting height, and room dimensions. HorusEyes 2.0 now explicitly reports `is_scale_uncalibrated = True` until paired with LiDAR or stereo ground truth.
